# Pnyx AI Disclosure Policy

Pnyx AI features assist with summaries, tags, related discussions, source suggestions, drafting, translation, and moderation signals. They do not establish truth or replace human judgment.

Generated or AI-assisted content must display its status, preserve the source material and contributing authors, and provide a correction/report path. AI output is never automatically marked verified. Private, restricted, blocked, or deleted content must not be included in AI context or retrieval without authorization.

Pnyx stores the provider, model, policy version, generation time, source identifiers, and output status for durable AI-assisted outputs. Users may correct outputs and report harmful or inaccurate results.
