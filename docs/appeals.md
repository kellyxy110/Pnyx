# Pnyx Appeals Process

Users may appeal a moderation action through the notice attached to that action. An appeal should identify the action, explain the relevant context, and include any evidence that was unavailable during the original review.

A moderator who made the original decision should not be the sole reviewer of the appeal. Appeals are recorded, reviewed against the applicable policy, and resolved with a concise explanation. Repeated abuse of the appeal process may be rate-limited, but a good-faith appeal must remain available.
