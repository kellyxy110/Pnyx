# Pnyx Community Guidelines

Pnyx is a place for useful technology conversation. Be specific, generous, and willing to revise your view.

- Treat people with respect. Harassment, threats, hate, doxxing, impersonation, and targeted abuse are not welcome.
- Share work honestly. Attribute sources, distinguish experience from speculation, and do not present generated material as verified expertise.
- Keep content relevant to its Space and help newcomers find the context they need.
- Do not spam, manipulate reactions, scrape private content, or use automation to overwhelm a community.
- Respect licenses, privacy, intellectual property, and responsible disclosure practices.
- Report problems rather than escalating them in public threads. Moderation decisions can be appealed through the documented process.
