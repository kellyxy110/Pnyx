# Pnyx Content Policy

Allowed content includes technology questions, explanations, research, benchmarks, tutorials, project showcases, professional learning, and constructive critique.

Disallowed content includes illegal exploitation, credible threats, harassment, hate, non-consensual personal information, malware distribution without legitimate defensive context, spam, fraud, impersonation, and plagiarism.

Moderators may label, limit, remove, or escalate content. Enforcement should be proportionate, recorded in the audit log, and explained to the affected user when safe. Safety-critical technical information should include context and responsible-use boundaries.
